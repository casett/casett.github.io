I am a Senior Computational Biologist at Barnwell Bio, where I use environmental biosurveillance and metagenomics to improve animal health. I came to this work through a decade of microbiome and metagenomics research, including a NSF postdoctoral fellowship with Dr. Jason Stajich at the University of California, Riverside.

Dissertation Research

For my dissertation with Dr. Jonathan Eisen at the University of California, Davis, I investigated the taxonomic diversity of seagrass-associated bacteria (aka the seagrass microbiome) and fungi (aka the seagrass mycobiome).

Unravelling the evolutionary patterns of symbiosis

Our latest work, "Assessing the Evolutionary Trajectory of Arbuscular Mycorrhizal Conserved Genes in Seagrasses and Aquatic Close Relatives" is out in Aquatic Botany.
